# Intent — Web3 Participation Loop

## 1. Why This Problem?

The fictional platform acquires participants through open acquisition channels (direct, referral, campaign, community, partner, social, other) into a waitlist and moves them through a project-disclosure and review journey. Entry does not require a personal invitation — the acquisition channel is recorded for attribution and does not determine eligibility.

The business objective is not simply to increase registrations, disclosure views, wallet connections or raw review submissions. The valuable outcome is meaningful participation that produces a useful review and can create a legitimate business follow-up.

I considered four bottlenecks:

1. Acquisition → waitlist activation
2. Waitlist → disclosure engagement
3. Disclosure/review → qualified reviewer
4. Qualified reviewer → business follow-up

I selected **disclosure/review → qualified reviewer** as the focus of this Quest.

The selected bottleneck provides a clear connection between user value and business conversion while allowing the operating model to define qualification, incentives, duplicate handling, abuse rules, measurement and optional on-chain acknowledgement.

## 2. Bottlenecks Considered

| Bottleneck                           | User Value | Business Conversion | Confidence | Effort | Total |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------: | ------------------: | ---------: | -----: | ----: |
| Acquisition → Waitlist               |          3 |                   4 |          3 |      4 |     9 |
| Waitlist → Disclosure                |          4 |                   3 |          2 |      3 |     8 |
| Disclosure/Review → Qualified Review |          5 |                   5 |          4 |      4 |    25 |
| Qualified Review → Follow-up         |          4 |                   5 |          3 |      3 |    20 |

Scores use a 1–5 scale and represent prioritization assumptions for the fictional assessment, calculated as:
Value × Conversion × Confidence ÷ Effort

## 3. Selected Bottleneck

**Disclosure/review → qualified reviewer**

A raw review submission is not necessarily useful participation.

Submissions may be:

- incomplete;
- generic;
- unrelated to the disclosure;
- duplicated;
- abusive;
- generated by synthetic bot participants.

The prototype therefore creates an explicit qualification layer between raw participation and business conversion.

## 4. Affected Users

### Acquired Participant

A person who arrives through an acquisition channel and may join the review waitlist.

### Waitlisted Participant

An acquired person who has registered for the review opportunity.

### Reviewer

A waitlisted participant who enters the disclosure/review flow.

### Qualified Reviewer

A reviewer whose submission satisfies the qualification rules.

### Business / Partner User

A user who can act on qualified reviews through legitimate follow-up activity.

### Operations User

An internal operator responsible for reviewing submissions, handling exceptions and monitoring funnel performance.

## 5. Evidence

This Quest is based on the fictional scenario supplied in the assessment brief.

No external user interviews, production analytics, campaign results or customer claims are being represented as evidence.

The prototype uses synthetic participants and simulated outcomes.

## 6. Intended User Value

Participants should be able to:

- access and understand the project disclosure;
- identify a useful question or observation;
- submit meaningful feedback;
- participate without connecting a wallet;
- optionally record a participation acknowledgement.

## 7. Intended Business Value

The operating loop should help the platform:

- increase qualified reviewer participation;
- improve review quality;
- identify useful business follow-up opportunities;
- measure acquisition and participation channels;
- distinguish meaningful participation from vanity metrics;
- maintain clear separation between off-chain participation and optional on-chain acknowledgement.

## 8. Core Product Principle

The platform should optimize for meaningful user actions and business conversion rather than:

- follower count;
- raw transactions;
- wallet connections;
- raw submissions.

A wallet acknowledgement is a separate activity and is not itself a qualified business conversion.

## 9. Non-Goals

This Quest does not attempt to:

- process real money;
- issue real rewards;
- conduct a token sale;
- promise TGE allocation;
- promise an airdrop;
- provide yield;
- verify asset ownership;
- verify reserves;
- deploy production smart contracts;
- execute mainnet transactions;
- conduct live user research;
- run a live campaign;
- collect real customer data;
- build production-grade Sybil detection.

## 10. Key Assumptions

1. The platform can acquire participants into the waitlist through open acquisition channels.
2. Participants can access the fictional disclosure.
3. Participants can submit structured reviews.
4. A review can be evaluated against explicit qualification rules.
5. Business follow-up can be represented as an operational event.
6. Wallet acknowledgement can be simulated.
7. All participants and outcomes in the prototype are synthetic.
